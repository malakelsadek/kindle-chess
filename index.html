Settings

Theme
Light or dark, both kept high-contrast for e-ink.
Mode
Play the built-in computer, or pass the device back and forth for local two-player.
Difficulty
How far ahead the computer searches. Lower levels also throw in the occasional random move so they play weaker, not just slower. Ratings are rough estimates.
You play
Your side against the computer; the board flips to match.
Board markings
Rank/file letters and numbers around the board edge.
Piece theme
Unicode text glyphs (default, works everywhere), or vector art (Staunty, Lichess) if your browser supports it.
